Jongshon ("the app", "we") shows where Bangladesh Railway trains are and when they will reach your station. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Jongshon is an independent app. **It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Bangladesh Railway.**

## Summary

- You do not need an account. We never ask for your name, phone number or email to use the app.
- Your location is used **only while you choose to share your train's position**, and only to place the train on the map. Other people see the train, never you.
- Raw location points are deleted after **7 days**.
- We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

## What we collect and why

| Data | When | Why | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Device ID**: a random identifier created when you install the app | Always | To count each phone once, limit abuse, and remember your saved trains and alerts on our server | Until you delete the app's data or ask us to delete it |
| **Precise location** (GPS position, speed, direction, accuracy, time) | Only while you have turned on "I'm on this train" sharing | To work out where the train is, its delay and arrival times for everyone | Raw points: 7 days. After that we keep only the time the train passed each station, with no link to your device |
| **Train and destination you chose** | While sharing | To match your position to the right train and stop sharing automatically at your stop | Same as location |
| **Station reports** ("the train just arrived") and pasted TTMS replies | When you send one | To update the train's position | 30 days, then only the station event is kept |
| **Chat messages and the nickname you pick** | When you post in a train's chat | To show your message to other passengers on that train | Chat rooms close 24 hours after the journey ends; messages are deleted after [TBD] days |
| **Saved trains, stations and alert settings** | When you set them | To send the alerts you asked for | Until you remove them |
| **Push notification token** | If you allow notifications | To deliver alerts about your trains | Until you turn notifications off or uninstall |
| **Crash reports and basic diagnostics** (app version, phone model, Android version, error details) | When the app crashes | To find and fix bugs | 90 days |

We do **not** collect your contacts, photos, files, SMS messages or call logs. The "Track via SMS" button only opens your own messaging app; Jongshon cannot read your messages. If you paste an SMS reply into the app, only the text you paste is sent.

## Location in the background

When you turn on sharing, Android shows a permanent notification while Jongshon uses your location, including when the screen is off. Sharing stops when you tap **Stop**, when you reach the destination you chose, or after [TBD] hours, whichever comes first. You can also revoke location permission at any time in Android settings.

## Who we share data with

We share data only with services that run the app for us, and only what they need:

- **Hosting**: our own servers at [TBD: provider and country].
- **Push notifications**: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Expo push service, which receive your push token and the alert text.
- **Crash reporting**: Sentry, which receives crash reports.
- **Map tiles**: map images are downloaded from [TBD: tile host]; the request includes your IP address, as with any website.

Other users see only a train's combined position, delay and estimated arrival, never an individual's location or device. We may disclose information if required by Bangladeshi law.

## Your choices and rights

- Turn sharing off at any time; nothing is collected about your location when sharing is off.
- Remove saved trains, alerts and chat messages from within the app.
- **Delete everything**: Settings → Privacy → Delete my data removes everything linked to your device ID from our servers within 30 days. You can also email [TBD contact] with the device ID shown in Settings.
- Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on your phone.

## Children

Jongshon is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our servers is restricted. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and show a notice in the app before the change applies.
